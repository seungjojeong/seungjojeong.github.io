Seungjo Jeong

Artist and researcher, London

Seungjo Jeong is a London-based artist and researcher, born in South Korea in 1977, with a background in software engineering. He studied at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago (BFA, 2015) and the Royal College of Art, London (MA Painting, 2017; PhD in Arts and Humanities, 2025). His doctoral research, Painting Practice as Network: The Interoperability of Contemporary Painting, reconsiders the relation between a painting and its on-screen image. He has exhibited in Bloomberg New Contemporaries at the Institute of Contemporary Arts, London (2016), the John Moores Painting Prize at the Walker Art Gallery, Liverpool, as part of the Liverpool Biennial (2018), and the Contemporary British Painting Prize at Huddersfield Art Gallery (2022). His awards include the New Contemporaries Studio Bursary, in partnership with One Thoresby Street, Nottingham (2018), and the British Institution Awards for Students, Royal Academy of Arts, London (2019).
